# 08 — Analytics

The Analytics section provides an aggregated view of all sustainability data indexed by the Atlas. Rather than focusing on an individual project, issuance, methodology, or registry, Analytics combines information across the entire dataset to reveal market-wide trends, supply characteristics, project progress, and sustainability impact.

## The stakeholder dashboards

The Analytics page is organized into several stakeholder-focused dashboards:

* **Market Overview** – overall market activity, project lifecycle, issuance trends, and market composition.
* **Buyer View** – supply availability, vintage profile, methodology adoption, and SDG coverage from a purchaser's perspective.
* **Developer View** – developer performance, project scale, and workflow distribution.
* **Registry View** – registry activity, throughput, market share, and project pipeline.
* **Climate Impact** – SDG alignment, sector contributions, country distribution, and environmental impact indicators.
